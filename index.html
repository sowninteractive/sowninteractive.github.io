About the studio

The best experiences grow from small, honest ideas. We give those ideas the tools, time, and care to become something people love.

Founded in 2025, Sown Interactive is a small team of designers, engineers, and artists spread across three continents. We share a love of craft and a belief that the best work happens when you respect the person on the other side of the screen.

Every project we ship is playtested by real people, built in the open where we can, and supported long after launch. We don't chase trends. We grow things.

Our vision: simple, intuitive tools that grow into rich, immersive experiences, so anyone with a spark of creativity can bring it to life.

Roots in the mud

Potential is all you need to begin.

Picture a great old oak. You look at it and think, damn, I want one of those. So you get an acorn, and you’re excited.

Then the people around you roast it. It’s a pathetic little acorn. It isn’t a tree yet, and you can’t do anything with it. You might even feel like you’re at rock bottom, buried alive right next to it.

That’s how it starts. You’re down in the mud, just surviving, and the seed is down there with you: underground, overlooked, doubted by plenty. But as long as it’s still there, it’s going to grow.

Eventually it sprouts, and the conditions are rarely kind. The soil is tough. The rain doesn’t come when it should, and the light isn’t quite right. Maybe just as it starts to branch out, some maniac with a machete comes along and chops pieces off.

It still grows.

All that pressure and stress makes the final tree stronger. It’s called high-stress training.

Great things have humble beginnings.

So when you’re developing yourself and your dreams and the pressure hits, know this: you will come out stronger than ever. Potential, when it’s believed in and pursued, can become something amazing. The challenges aren’t stopping you. They’re making the result better.
