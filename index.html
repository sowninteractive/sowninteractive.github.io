About the studio

No bloat. No chasing trends. Just an independent studio building games, apps, and websites from the ground up.

Founded in 2026, Sown Interactive is an independent solo studio building video games, browser-based creative tools, and purposeful websites. Every project is designed, coded, and shipped from the ground up.

Everything here is built in the open - from early prototypes to finished tools you can use right in your browser. Rather than chasing trends or bloated scope, the focus stays on solving real problems and shipping work that lasts.

Our vision: simple, intuitive tools that grow into rich, immersive experiences, so anyone with a spark of creativity can bring it to life.

Roots in the mud

Potential is all you need to begin.

Picture a great old oak. You look at it and think, I want one of those. So you get an acorn, and you’re excited.

Then people around you criticize it. It’s a pathetic little acorn. It isn’t a tree yet, and it doesn't look like much. You might even feel like you’re at rock bottom, buried in the dirt right beside it.

That’s how it starts. You’re down in the mud, just surviving, and the seed is down there with you: underground, overlooked, doubted by plenty. But as long as it’s still there, it’s going to grow.

Eventually it breaks through, and the conditions are rarely kind. The soil is tough. The rain doesn’t come when it should, and the light isn’t quite right. Maybe just as it starts to branch out, some maniac with a machete comes along and chops pieces off.

It still grows.

All that pressure and resistance makes the final tree stronger. In gardening, it’s called high-stress training - bending and stressing a plant so it grows back better.

Great things have humble beginnings.

That’s the story behind Sown Interactive—and behind every dream worth building. Potential, when believed in and pursued, becomes something undeniable. The resistance isn't stopping you; it's making the result stronger.
